Projects

KeroKero - 2025

KeroKero is a short tower defense game about various frog creatures protecting their realm from other creatures. Inspired by The Battle Cats.
Features:

  • 5 unique units
  • Unit upgrade system
  • 4 levels of increasing difficulty
  • Jukebox to listen to the soundtrack after you've encountered it in game
  • Charming pixel art

This was a final major project made for college made by a team of 3 people as well as being my first real project. My role on the team was to be a generalist programmer as my teammate specialised in art.
This means that I interacted with many systems such as: UI placement and anchoring, state machines and saving data between scenes.

Cannon Clash - 2026

Cannon Clash is a short strategy game about taking down pirate ships to claim their loot. Inspired by Plants Vs. Zombies. Made for GBJam14.
Features:

  • 5 units with different purposes
  • CPU placement system that adapts to your strategy
  • 5 levels with the CPU getting smarter after each level
  • Soundtrack made by YaboMusic
  • Themed around the original gameboy using the Unity pixel perfect camera
  • Pixel art made with the limitation of only being 4 colours

This was a quick game jam project made by a team of 3 people in 10 days. The theme was around old gold. My role on the team was making sprites as well as implementing an animation system using lists of sprites
as well as programming all of the different towers seen in game.
